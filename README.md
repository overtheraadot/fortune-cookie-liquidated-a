A make.fun entry for "Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated" — one fortune cookie line, at most 20 words, for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated.

## The entry

> Your portfolio will rise again; the next trader's will, on the coins you sold at the bottom.

92 characters, 17 words, 1 line. The fortune keeps its promise — the portfolio really will rise again — it just hands the happy ending to the next trader, the one who bought the coins the liquidated trader sold at the bottom.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 160 characters, not only white space, a single line, at most 20 words — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
